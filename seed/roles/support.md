# Customer Support Lead

## Job description
Larkspur Home is hiring a Customer Support Lead for the team that answers its online shoppers, about 20,000 contacts a month by chat, email and phone.

You will lead a team of agents, handle the hardest customer cases yourself, and run the ticketing platform that organizes the work. You will coach agents, report on how the team is doing, and work with the product and shipping teams to remove the causes of repeat contacts.

What you will do:
- Answer and de-escalate difficult customer cases with a calm, clear voice.
- Configure and improve the ticketing platform: queues, routing, templates and automation.
- Hire, coach and review a team of about ten agents.
- Track first response time, resolution time and satisfaction, and report them every month.
- Keep the help center accurate and turn common questions into fixes.

What we look for: strong customer communication, hands-on experience with a ticketing platform, and some experience leading people. Metrics and process improvement depth is a plus, not a requirement.

## Criterion: Customer communication and de-escalation
kind: must_have
weight: 5
0: No evidence of handling customer contacts.
1: Mentions customer-facing work only in passing, with no support cases described.
2: Answered customer chats, emails or calls following scripts, and passed difficult cases on to a manager.
3: Handled escalated and upset customers independently, with good satisfaction results.
4: Set the standard for de-escalation across a team, with a guide or training and a measured drop in complaints or repeat contacts.

## Criterion: Ticketing platform and workflow
kind: must_have
weight: 4
0: No evidence of using a ticketing platform or structured queues.
1: Mentions a helpdesk tool only in a skills list or has used a shared inbox.
2: Used a ticketing platform daily, with saved replies or views made by others.
3: Configured queues, routing rules and templates for a team, with measurable time saved.
4: Chose and rolled out a platform, with automation and integrations, and trained a large team on it.

## Criterion: Team leadership and coaching
kind: must_have
weight: 3
0: No evidence of leading or coaching anyone.
1: Mentions helping new colleagues informally.
2: Led a small group, assigned work or onboarded new hires, with reviews run by someone else.
3: Managed a team with one-to-ones, call or ticket reviews and hiring, and promoted or retained people.
4: Led several leads or a large team, with a coaching program and measured gains in retention or promotion.

## Criterion: Support metrics and reporting
kind: nice_to_have
weight: 2
0: No evidence of using support numbers.
1: Mentions counting cases or reading reports without detail.
2: Produced a regular report of volume or response time from existing tools.
3: Owned a scorecard of response time, resolution and satisfaction, and used it to make decisions.
4: Built the metrics pack and staffing forecast for a support organization, with measured savings or service gains.

## Criterion: Process improvement and knowledge base
kind: nice_to_have
weight: 1
0: No evidence of improving support processes or writing help content.
1: Mentions editing a few help pages or noticing repeated questions.
2: Wrote help articles or macros for common questions.
3: Ran root cause reviews or owned the help center and cut ticket volume with the fixes.
4: Led a recurring program with product and operations teams, with measured drops in contacts and a maintained knowledge base.
