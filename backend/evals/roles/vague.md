# Operations Person

## Job description
We are a fast growing company looking for a self-starter who gets things done. You will wear many hats and work with great people. We move quickly, so we need someone who can hit the ground running and make an impact. Culture fit matters. Tell us why you are the one.

## Criterion: Getting things done
kind: must_have
weight: 3
0: No evidence.
1: Mentioned in passing, with no examples.
2: Did it with guidance on a small scope.
3: Did it independently and owned the result.
4: Led others and showed measured results.
